About Our Institution

Where Joyful Curiosity Meets Academic Excellence

Established with a vision to transform schooling into an exciting adventure, Learnify Public School empowers young minds in Ganj Basoda through value-oriented guidance and hands-on discovery.

Foundational Stage Pedagogy

Specialized Foundational Wing for Early Learners

At Learnify Public School, our dedicated early-childhood learning environment is custom-designed for Play Group, Nursery, LKG, and UKG learners to ignite innate curiosity.

Our child-centric curriculum prioritizes cognitive brain development, sensory awareness, linguistic phonetics, and gross/fine motor skills. Children do not merely memorize—they touch, build, explore, and conceptualize within an emotionally secure, joyous environment.

  • Scientifically calibrated multi-sensory toys and brain-mapping puzzles
  • Phonics-based linguistic immersion fostering fluent bilingual expression
  • Warm, motherly care with dedicated child-to-teacher attention

Campus Infrastructure
